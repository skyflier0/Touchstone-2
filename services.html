Classes at Riverbend: something for every body and every schedule

Gentle

Gentle is our foundation class, built for beginners and anyone who prefers a slower pace. Every pose can be done seated on a chair or with blocks and a strap, and the teacher circulates one-on-one so you are never left guessing.

Vinyasa

Vinyasa Basics links breath to movement in a flowing sequence that fits into a lunch break or an early morning. It is a true vinyasa class kept at a welcoming pace, and busy professionals make up most of the room.

Restorative

Restorative is the opposite of a workout: supported poses held for several minutes with bolsters and blankets while the nervous system downshifts. Older adults love it because it is deep rest without strain, and it is our most gentle class of the week.

Private sessions

Prefer to start one-on-one? Private sessions are a good fit if you are returning from injury, managing a limited range of motion, or simply want the first weeks to yourself. Sessions are 45 minutes with Maya and can be held in the studio or over video call.

What it costs

Classes are $18 drop-in or four weeks unlimited for $120, and we never want cost to be the reason someone sits this out. There is no online checkout: pay at the front desk any time before class, or ask about our pay-what-you-can community rate when you request a spot on the Events page.
