Who runs Riverbend

Maya Chen, founder and lead teacher

Maya opened Riverbend in 2019 after spending years in big-box studios where she felt beginners got left behind. She is a 500-hour registered yoga teacher with additional training in chair yoga and working with older adults, and she still teaches the Tuesday Gentle class herself.

Access and accommodations

The studio is on the ground floor with a wide, step-free doorway and an accessible restroom down the hall. Chairs are available in every class at no extra cost, props are free to borrow, and hearing aid compatible speakers run for guided relaxations. Tell us what you need when you sign up and we will have it ready.

Studio policies

Please cancel drop-ins at least four hours ahead so the mat can go to the waitlist. Arrive ten minutes early for your first visit and we will show you around and set up your props. Bring a water bottle and anything you usually sit on; we provide mats, blocks, straps and blankets.
