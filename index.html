Riverbend Yoga Studio: gentle movement for every body

Our mission

Riverbend is a small neighborhood studio built for people who thought yoga was not for them. We teach calm, supportive classes where beginners, busy professionals, and older adults can move at their own pace and leave feeling better than they arrived.

New to yoga?

You do not need to touch your toes, and no one will ask you to. Our Gentle classes use chairs and props so every pose works for your body, and our teachers coach new students personally in every session. Come ten minutes early and we will walk you through everything.

Featured class: Restorative Sunday

Restorative Sunday is our favorite hour of the week: six supported poses held with bolsters and blankets, dim lights, and a long guided relaxation at the end. It is fully beginner friendly and the class most students return for week after week.

Weekly schedule

  • Thursday 7:30 am - Vinyasa Basics (for busy schedules, one hour)
  • Sunday 9:00 am - Restorative Sunday (slow, supported, quiet)
